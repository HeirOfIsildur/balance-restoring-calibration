# DIGEST: simulation in which the theorems' hypotheses hold exactly (tools/hypothesis_exact_sim.py, 11 Sep 2026)

**Design.** Cells (x, Y) are sampled i.i.d. from the closed-form per-development-year laws of the generator with the frailty integrated out (x = (c, k, r, prev), at most 48 support points per DY, log-scale variance sigma^2 = s^2 + tau^2 = 1.25). Every target, variance, predictor-weighted law, density ratio and bound is computed exactly on that support, so the references carry no Monte Carlo error. Scenarios S-A and S-B (S-C omitted: A3 fails there by construction). J = 3, R = 2000, n in {600, 3000, 15000}, nominal 95 %. Full run: 143 s wall on 8 workers.

**Law check.** One 200k-claim portfolio per scenario from `generate_dataset(seed=0, n_per_ay=20000)`: 1,406 z-scores (joint (c, r) law, accident-year composition, E[Y], E[oracle], P(Y>0) per DY and per (c, k, r), upper and lower triangle), max |z| = 3.64, none above 4, after setting aside 34 sparse-event groups (< 5 expected active cells; the only two |z| > 4 are Material/DY 10/r = 1 with 0.3 and 2.4 expected active cells, where a sample mean is Poisson-like and z is not normal). Moment formulas agree with adaptive quadrature to 4e-16 and with a 1e7-sample Monte Carlo draw within |z| <= 1.8.

**Panel A (Theorem 4.6(i), fixed predictors; random folds, DY-pooled unless stated).** With the exact V_n the interval covers alpha_n in 0.95-0.96 of replicates at every n for f1 and in 0.91/0.94/0.95 for f2 (heavier-tailed residual): the limit law is right. With the plug-in SE coverage is 0.82-0.91 (n = 600), 0.90-0.94 (3000), 0.93-0.95 (15000) and mean SE / empirical s.d. rises from 0.72-0.91 to 0.97-0.99: the pooled residual variance is biased low in small samples of heavy-tailed payments, worst at the late DYs (DY 10, n = 600: 0.78). In S-A the AY blocks coincide with random folds by construction (P_{a,k} does not depend on a inside the upper triangle); in S-B they differ, and at n = 15000, DY 4, f2, coverage of alpha_n is 0.946 against 0.880 for the pooled alpha_P (3.462 vs 3.563, 0.56 SE apart): the interval covers the equal-weight stratified target, not the pooled one.

**Panel A' (unequal deterministic fold sizes).** Coverage of alpha^w_n with the exact V^w is 0.945-0.959 at all n and s.d.(sqrt(n)(alpha_hat - alpha^w_n)) / sqrt(V^w) = 0.98-1.07, so the variance formula V^w = sum_j w_j Var_{P_j}(Y - alpha^w f) / (sum_j w_j m_j)^2 is confirmed; plug-in coverage 0.884-0.913 -> 0.939-0.948. At the nominal weights w_j = pi_j the target equals alpha_P to <= 1.8e-15 (both printed to 12 digits in run.log); integer rounding of the allocation moves the finite-sample target by < 1e-3. In S-B the equal-weight alpha_n is covered less often (0.906 at n = 15000, proportional shares, f1) than alpha^w_n (0.939): the target moves with the fold shares.

**Panel B (Corollary 4.12(iii), pure DY mix shift).** Identity residual 4.4e-16; per-DY population balance 2.2e-16. The pooled factor's exact relative error on the lower-triangle DY mix is +46.0 % (S-A) and +50.2 % (S-B) (+27.9 % / +32.2 % with DY 1 excluded from the pooled factor, the variant satisfying the positivity hypothesis at every group). Sampled over DY 1-10 (vs the population aggregate): pooled +46.9 / +45.6 / +46.0 % (S-A) and +49.1 / +50.1 / +50.0 % (S-B) at n = 600 / 3000 / 15000; per-DY -16.6 / -2.8 / -1.0 % and -15.9 / -4.7 / -1.3 %. Over DY 2-10 (every hypothesis holds): sampled pooled +30.0 / +27.6 / +27.7 % (S-A), +31.3 / +32.4 / +31.9 % (S-B); per-DY -12.4 / -3.8 / -0.9 % and -15.6 / -4.1 / -0.8 %. The per-DY finite-sample bias at n = 600 comes from the late DYs (about 11 training cells at DY 10, whose payments are dominated by the 3 % Annuity type).

**Panel C (Theorem 4.11(i),(iii), within-DY shifts of P_k, exact).** Identity residual <= 3.6e-15. Generator's own lower-triangle law, f1: |alpha_Q - alpha_P| = 0.073 / 0.057 / 0.044 at DY 3 / 5 / 7 in S-A (the P-factor over-predicts the Q-aggregate by 6.4-7.8 %) and 0.076 / 0.115 / 0.124 in S-B (under-predicts by 6.7-15.1 %); the chi^2 bound is 1.7-3.2 times and the TV bound 2.0-3.7 times the actual discrepancy. For the parametric type-mix shifts (f1) the chi^2 bound is within 3-7 % of the actual (ratio 1.03-1.07) and the TV bound 1.25-1.58 times; for the reporting-delay shifts both are loose by one to two orders of magnitude (chi^2 6.6-113 times). For f2, whose miscalibration r = g/f2 spans orders of magnitude, the bounds are loose by up to 2,400 (chi^2) and 8,500 (TV) times.

**Panel D (Theorem 4.6(iv), cross-fitted cell-mean learners; the stability assumption holds exactly).** Coverage at the cross-fitted target alpha_n with the plug-in SE: log-target learner 0.863 / 0.909 / 0.941 (S-A, random folds), 0.901 / 0.932 / 0.949 (AY blocks); raw learner 0.861 / 0.911 / 0.934; with the exact limit variance V, 0.92-0.95 (log). Coverage at alpha_inf: log learner 0.43 / 0.49 / 0.59 (random). The centring term is large: mean m_hat_bar / m_inf_bar - 1 = 2.8 (n = 600), 0.84 (3000), 0.21 (15000) over DY 2-9 (2.4 % at DY 2 but 76 % at DY 9 at n = 15000). the retransformed cell mean carries a positive finite-sample bias, largest on the thin heavy Annuity cells (1-100 training cells), which carry most of the predictor mass, and the integrated predictor error's training-sample fluctuation is O_p(n^{-1/2}) with positive limiting variance, so the o_p(n^{-1/2}) centring condition of Theorem 4.6(iv) fails although A4 holds. alpha_inf itself is 3.8-36.6 (the Jensen factor of the log target); the mean alpha_hat / alpha_inf is 0.74 (n = 3000) and 0.88 (15000). Coverage at the deployed-predictor target alpha^pop: log 0.72 / 0.78 / 0.865 (random), 0.79 / 0.88 / 0.92 (blocks, S-A), 0.78 / 0.82 / 0.83 (blocks, S-B); (alpha^pop - alpha_n)/SE = 0.2-1.0 at n = 15000 because the full-sample predictor has a smaller retransformation bias than the 2n/3-cell fold predictors, so alpha^pop > alpha_n systematically. Raw learner: coverage at alpha_inf = 1 is 0.999-1.000: the cross-fitted ratio of the raw cell-mean learner is close to 1 at large n (exactly 1 with equal within-cell fold counts; its mean is 1.20 in S-A and 1.80 in S-B at n = 600), so alpha_hat - alpha_inf is far smaller than the SE while alpha_hat - alpha_n has the theorem's variance; coverage at alpha^pop 0.87 / 0.91 / 0.93.

**Surprises and caveats.** (1) Even with every hypothesis exact, the plug-in interval under-covers at n = 600-3000 because of the heavy right tail of Y; the exact-variance interval does not, so the shortfall is mostly variance estimation, the normal approximation contributing for the heavier-tailed predictor f2 at n = 600 (exact-V coverage 0.91-0.92). (2) For the log-target learner the cross-fitted centre alpha_n and the limit / deployed centres differ by up to 1 SE at n = 15000: the 92 % coverage at the deployed target in Table S5 has a clean population analogue here. (3) The raw cell-mean learner's cross-fitted ratio is close to 1 at large n (exactly 1 with equal within-cell fold counts). (4) The chi^2 bound is near-tight for a type-mix shift and loose by orders of magnitude for shifts weakly aligned with the miscalibration. (5) 15 of 2000 replicates at S-B, DY 10, n = 600 had no positive payment (alpha_hat = 0, degenerate interval counted as a miss); 1 of 2000 at S-B, DY 9, n = 600 left both cell-mean learners identically zero (recorded as undefined, the event Theorem 4.6 excludes; this crashed the first attempt, run_crashed_attempt1.log). (6) The AY-block designs exist only for DY 1-4 with the blocks {1,2,3}, {4,5,6}, {7,...}; the cells are i.i.d. pairs with a fresh frailty each, so the reduced covariate carries no payment history and the experiment is the theorems' setting, not the pipeline's feature map.
